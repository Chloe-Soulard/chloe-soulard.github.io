Member of the organising committee of the [2026 INRAE BAP division PhD and postdoc days](https://ijpb.versailles.inrae.fr/en/news/2026-inrae-bap-division-phd-and-postdoc-days-organised-by-the-ijpb), 27–29 May 2026, INRAE Versailles: a three-day meeting for 56 early-career researchers of the INRAE Plant Biology and Breeding division (BAP), with talks, flash talks and poster sessions, organised by a committee of PhD students and postdocs from IJPB. As part of the programme, we held a round table with private companies.
