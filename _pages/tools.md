Tools I built for my own research and share in case they are useful to others. Each one is a single HTML file that runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

## [Alleloscope](https://github.com/Chloe-Soulard/Drawing-genes-alleles-and-protein-consequences)
Draws publication-ready figures of genes, their alleles and the consequences on the protein.

## [Chromatoscope](https://github.com/Chloe-Soulard/Chromatoscope)
Compares several Sanger sequencing chromatograms (.ab1) side by side to check alleles at a glance. Files are read locally and never leave your computer.
