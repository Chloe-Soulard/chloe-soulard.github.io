Hi, I'm Chloé! I am a PhD student in plant biology at [Université Paris-Saclay](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/), working jointly at the [Institut Jean-Pierre Bourgin (IJPB)](https://ijpb.versailles.inrae.fr/) of INRAE in Versailles, and at the Plant Health Laboratory of [ANSES](https://www.anses.fr/). At IJPB, I work in two teams: [DNA Repair and Genome Engineering (DRAGON)](https://ijpb.versailles.inrae.fr/en/research-teams/dna-repair-and-genome-engineering) and Strigolactones and Allelochemicals Signaling (SAS).

PhD project
======
**Editing of strigolactone biosynthesis genes in pea for the modulation of rhizospheric interactions and assessment of associated risks** ([theses.fr](https://theses.fr/s396557)), started in September 2024 under the supervision of Fabien Nogué.

Using CRISPR-Cas9, I generate pea mutants with changes in the amount and type of strigolactones they produce, to study how these changes affect interactions with soil microorganisms and responses to pathogens. I also study the molecular consequences of genome-editing tools in pea, to inform risk assessment and the traceability of edited plants.

Research interests
======
My current research focuses on pea (*Pisum sativum*); I previously worked with the moss *Physcomitrium patens*. My main interests are:

- **Genome editing**: developing and applying CRISPR-Cas9 tools in pea, a legume crop that has long been difficult to edit ([Soulard et al., 2025](/publication/2025-crispr-cas9-pisum-sativum)).
- **Strigolactone biosynthesis**: understanding how pea produces strigolactones, plant hormones that shape plant architecture and act as signals in the soil.

Background
======
I trained as an engineer at the [Université de Technologie de Compiègne (UTC)](https://www.utc.fr/), including an exchange semester at [TU Braunschweig](https://www.tu-braunschweig.de/) in Germany. During my studies, I did a research internship at the ECOBIO lab in Rennes with Céline Roose-Amsaleg, on the inhibition of sewage sludge anaerobic digestion by propionic acid. I then joined IJPB for a second internship with Fabien Nogué and Pierre-François Perroud, on the cell-cycle regulation of Cas9 activity to improve homology-directed repair.
